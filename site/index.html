    Frequently asked questions

    Does applying affect my credit score?

    No. Kikoff doesn't run a hard credit check when you apply.

    Is there interest?

    No. Kikoff charges no interest and no hidden fees. You pay a simple monthly plan.

    How quickly can I see results?

    It depends on your profile. Many people see changes after a few months of on-time payments.
